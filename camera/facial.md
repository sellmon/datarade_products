### Description:  
The Facial Landmark Dataset is crafted to support developers, researchers, and AI teams in creating advanced facial landmark detection models. It contains **unique, up-to-date selfies** annotated with key points for facial features such as eyes, nose, mouth, jawline, and more. These annotations are carefully labeled to ensure precision and consistency across the dataset.  

Ideal for applications in augmented reality (AR), facial recognition systems, and beauty technology, this dataset helps you build robust AI models capable of accurately detecting and analyzing facial landmarks in real-world scenarios.  

Key features include:  
- **Fresh and Unique Selfies**: All photos are recently captured using mobile cameras, reflecting realistic conditions and diverse environments.  
- **Detailed Annotations**: Each image includes key points for critical facial landmarks such as the corners of the eyes, mouth, and nose bridge.  
- **Comprehensive Diversity**: Data includes individuals from various age groups, ethnicities, and facial structures, enabling better generalization for AI models.  
- **Customizable Key Points**: Additional annotation requirements, such as specific areas like cheeks, forehead, or chin, can be provided upon request.  
- **Optimized for AI Models**: Data is formatted for seamless integration with machine learning frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, and OpenCV.  
- **Free Sample Available**: Preview the dataset's quality before purchase.  

Whether you're building AR filters, training facial recognition systems, or working on beauty applications, this dataset provides the foundational data to develop precise and reliable models.
