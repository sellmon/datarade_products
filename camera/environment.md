### Title:  
Environmental Condition Dataset | Weather & Scene Analysis for AI Models | Indoor vs. Outdoor | Diverse Conditions  

### Short Description:  
The Environmental Condition Dataset includes high-quality, labeled images of various weather conditions and indoor/outdoor scenes. Ideal for AI training in environmental analysis, it features diverse scenarios to support applications like smart weather systems and environmental monitoring.  

### Description:  
The Environmental Condition Dataset provides **fresh and unique photos** of different environmental scenarios, covering a wide variety of weather conditions (sunny, rainy, snowy, foggy) and indoor vs. outdoor settings. This dataset is meticulously annotated to help AI models distinguish and analyze environmental contexts accurately.  

It’s designed for a range of applications, including smart weather monitoring, autonomous vehicle navigation, and scene classification for smart devices. The dataset is diverse, capturing images from different geographic regions, times of day, and environmental setups, ensuring robust AI model training.  

Key features include:  
- **Comprehensive Weather Conditions**: Images labeled with detailed weather types like clear skies, thunderstorms, snowfall, or fog.  
- **Indoor vs. Outdoor Scenes**: Labeled images that clearly differentiate between indoor and outdoor environments.  
- **Diverse Representation**: Photos captured from multiple global locations, times, and lighting conditions.  
- **Customizable Attributes**: Additional metadata such as temperature, time of day, and object presence can be included.  
- **AI-Ready Formats**: Delivered in widely-used formats for seamless integration with AI frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, or OpenCV.  
- **Free Sample Available**: Evaluate the dataset quality with a sample before purchasing.  

This dataset is ideal for training AI systems in weather recognition, environmental classification, and smart home or city technologies.
